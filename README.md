# Folsom Hack Club

This is the website for the Hack Club chapter at Folsom High School. It's where students can learn about the club, see when our next meeting is, meet the officers, and sign up to join.

We meet every other Monday from 3:40 to 4:30 PM in room F204. If you have any questions, you can reach us at fhshackclub@gmail.com or on Instagram at [@fhshack_club](https://instagram.com/fhshack_club). You can learn more about Hack Club as a whole at [hackclub.com](https://hackclub.com).

## How we built it

- **Next.js and React** for the pages and components, written in **TypeScript**.
- **Plain CSS**, with the colors and fonts taken from the [Hack Club brand guide](https://hackclub.com/brand) so the site matches the rest of Hack Club.
- **Firebase (Cloud Firestore)** to store responses from our interest form. The database is only accessed from the server, so sign-up information stays private.
- **Vercel** to host the site.

## What's on the site

- **Home:** An overview of the club, who it's for, when we meet, how to join, and photos from our meetings.
- **About:** Our officers, what the club is about, and what a typical meeting looks like.
- **Contact:** A contact form, our contact information, and answers to common questions.
- **Admin:** A password-protected page where officers can see everyone who has signed up.
